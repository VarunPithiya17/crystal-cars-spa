Questions

Good to know

Do you wash bikes and scooters too?

Yes — every two-wheeler from mopeds and scooters to superbikes. We use chain-safe degreasers and cover electricals before the foam.

How long does a ceramic coating take?

Usually 1–2 days including paint correction and curing. We'll give you an exact slot when you book.

Is the touchless wash really scratch-free?

High-pressure rinse and snow foam remove grit before anything touches the paint — so no swirl marks, safe for coated and PPF cars.

Ceramic vs Teflon vs PPF — which one do I need?

Teflon is an affordable 3–6 month gloss shield. Ceramic bonds for 1–3 years with deep water beading. PPF is a physical film that stops stone chips and scratches. Ask us — we'll recommend by budget and usage.

Can I pay for a yearly plan in instalments?

Yearly plans are paid once for the biggest saving; monthly plans are billed month to month. UPI, cards and cash accepted.
